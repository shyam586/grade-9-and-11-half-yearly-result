      How it is calculated: each subject mark is first converted to a score out of 100 (mark ÷ maximum marks × 100), and a student's average percentage is the mean of those scores, so 50-mark and 80-mark papers count equally. A subject is passed at 33% or more. Absent (AB) counts as zero and a fail; medical leave (ML) is left out. Subjects marked "-" were not taken. Students with missing marks () or no marks at all are counted in subject figures but excluded from percentages, ranks and pass rate.
